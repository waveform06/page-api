About Us

Local painters, decorators & refurbishment specialists

Sinclair Refurbishments & Decorating is run by Charlie Sinclair and based in Sutton, Surrey. From a single room refresh to a full refurbishment, every job gets the same careful preparation, clean working and attention to detail.

We work for both domestic and commercial customers, offer no-obligation appointments and quotes for jobs of any size, and have access to a trusted team of tradesmen when a project needs more than paint — including plastering, carpentry, tiling, brickwork and electrics.

What We Do

Painting, Decorating & Refurbishment Services

Everything from a fresh coat of paint to a complete home or office refurbishment, for homeowners, landlords and businesses across Sutton, Surrey and South West London.

INTERIOR DECORATING

Walls, ceilings, woodwork, stairs and landings — brightening rooms with clean lines and durable finishes.

EXTERIOR PAINTING

Render, windows, porches and guttering, with jet washing and thorough preparation before any paint goes on.

SPRAY PAINTING

Professional spray application for a smoother, factory-quality finish on walls, doors and joinery.

WALLPAPERING

Quality wall coverings, feature walls and murals hung neatly with perfectly matched patterns.

WINDOWS & TIMBER

Wooden windows and doors stripped and repainted, timber resin repairs and full timber restoration.

REFURBISHMENTS

Complete room, home and office refurbishments — including kitchen replacements — coordinated from start to finish.

PLASTERING & REPAIRS

Crack filling, making good and plastering so walls are smooth and ready for a flawless finish.

SPECIALIST FINISHES

Specialist paint effects, anti-vandal coatings, coving and bespoke finishes for distinctive spaces.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Sutton (SM3) and work across Sutton, Cheam, Worcester Park, Carshalton, Wallington, Banstead, Ewell, Epsom, Morden, Mitcham and Croydon, and throughout South West London including Wimbledon, Merton, Raynes Park, New Malden, Kingston, Surbiton, Wandsworth, Putney, Tooting, Balham, Streatham, Clapham and Battersea, as well as parts of South East London.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. We offer free, no-obligation estimates for jobs of any size. Call Charlie on 07540 487 044 or send an enquiry through the form on this page.

Do you only do painting and decorating, or full refurbishments too?

Both. Alongside interior and exterior painting and decorating, we carry out full home and office refurbishments, including kitchen replacements, and have a trusted team for plastering, carpentry, tiling, brickwork and electrical work.

Do you paint house exteriors?

Yes. We paint render, masonry, wooden windows, doors, porches and guttering, with jet washing and thorough preparation before painting.

Do you offer spray painting?

Yes. We use professional spray equipment for a smoother finish on walls, doors, kitchens and joinery.

Do you take on commercial work?

Yes. We work for both domestic and commercial customers, including office refurbishments and commercial redecoration.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry full public liability insurance, and we are vetted members of Checkatrade and TrustATrader with references available on request.
